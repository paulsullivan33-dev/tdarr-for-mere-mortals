t01 — The fixture videos, and their "before" state
============================================================
Three tiny invented videos, generated by ffmpeg's test patterns
(the generator script is tdarr-lab/make-fixtures.sh). No real
media, no real titles. The first two break the lab's rule —
"video shall be H.264" — in different ways; the third (added
later, in t05) already obeys it.

  $ ls -la media/
  -rw-rw---- 1  721048  Cooking Basics - Episode One.avi
  -rw-rw---- 1 1736532  Harbour Lights - Trailer.mkv

Before — probed with ffprobe (streams and file size only):

  == Cooking Basics - Episode One.avi
  stream|codec_name=mpeg4|codec_type=video
  stream|codec_name=mp3|codec_type=audio
  format|size=721048

  == Harbour Lights - Trailer.mkv
  stream|codec_name=mpeg2video|codec_type=video
  stream|codec_name=mp2|codec_type=audio
  format|size=1736532

So before Tdarr: MPEG-4 part 2 + MP3 in AVI, and MPEG-2 + MP2
in MKV. The after state is in t04.

Added for t05 (obeys the rule already):
